Quick answer

Note when the noise happens, clean filters, check the outdoor unit for debris and loose panels, and listen for grinding or banging. Hissing, screeching, or metal-on-metal needs appliance service switched off.

What usually causes this

The visible symptom is useful, but the small detail around it usually tells the real story.

  • Dirty filters making the indoor fan labour.
  • Leaves or debris hitting the outdoor fan.
  • Loose mounting screws or a vibrating cover panel.

Try these in order

Begin with the reversible checks. The goal is a clean fix, not winning an argument with a stubborn household thing.

Method 1

Note the pattern

Best for: any new noise

Write down whether it happens at startup, while running, or when the compressor stops.

Method 2

Clean the filters

Best for: weak airflow with buzz

Wash and fully dry filters before retesting.

Method 3

Inspect the outdoor unit

Best for: rattle or whir

Clear debris around the fan and check that panels sit flush.

Method 4

Check mounting and vibration

Best for: shaking indoor unit

Look for loose screws on covers; do not open electrical sections.

Method 5

Book appliance service

Best for: grinding, screech, or banging

A technician can test the fan motor, compressor mounts, and refrigerant safely.

Kashmir home note

Dusty summers clog filters quickly in Kashmir. A rattling AC after months of idle use often wants a filter wash and an outdoor clear-out before any major repair talk.

What not to do

These shortcuts usually cost more than they save.

Don't 1

Ignore grinding or banging

Metal-on-metal can mean a failing motor or mount

A loud bang or grind is not background noise; running through it can damage the fan or compressor.

Don't 2

Open panels with power on

Shock and warranty risk

Live covers expose moving parts and electrics. Switch off at the breaker before any panel check.

Don't 3

Assume every noise needs gas

Misdiagnosis wastes money

Rattles and buzzes often come from filters, debris, or loose panels — not refrigerant loss.

Keep it from returning

Fix the small trigger, keep the area clean and dry where relevant, and deal with repeat symptoms before they become a replacement job.

When to book a professional

Book appliance repair for metal grinding, loud banging, burning smell, tripping breaker, or noise with poor cooling.

Can I keep using it?

Only if there is no safety risk or spreading damage. Stop when the symptom worsens.

Why did this start suddenly?

A change in load, moisture, wear, cleaning product, or season often exposes a weak point.

Will a temporary fix last?
