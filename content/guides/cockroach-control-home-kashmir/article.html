Late at night, the kitchen light catches one cockroach crossing the counter before it slips behind a jar. It is small, quick, and very good at making a quiet room feel less peaceful.

One roach after lights-out is a clue, not a verdict. Follow the food, water and hiding route before reaching for a spray.

Quick answer

Remove food and water overnight, clean grease, place labelled gel bait only in hidden protected spots, then seal entry gaps after activity falls. Book pest control for daytime sightings, droppings, egg cases, or activity that spreads.

Methods that actually help — try them in this order

Start with the lowest-risk, reversible method. Move on only if it does not explain or improve the cockroach activity.

Method 1

Clean food traces and grease

Best for: fresh activity around the stove

  1. Empty crumbs, wipe grease with a suitable cleaner, move small appliances, and empty the bin before bed.
  2. Work in good light and take a photo before changing anything.
  3. Change one variable at a time so you can tell what helped.
  4. Stop if the issue worsens or exposes a safety concern.
Method 2

Remove overnight water

Best for: sink or under-sink sightings

  1. Dry the sink, hang cloths to dry, repair drips, and put pet water away overnight where practical.
  2. Work in good light and take a photo before changing anything.
  3. Change one variable at a time so you can tell what helped.
  4. Stop if the issue worsens or exposes a safety concern.
Method 3

Use gel bait exactly as labelled

Best for: hidden low-level activity

  1. Place small protected dots in cracks inaccessible to children and pets; never spray repellent over bait.
  2. Work in good light and take a photo before changing anything.
  3. Change one variable at a time so you can tell what helped.
  4. Stop if the issue worsens or exposes a safety concern.
Method 4

Seal routes after activity drops

Best for: gaps around pipes or cabinets

  1. Caulk narrow gaps and fit simple pipe collars only after bait has had time to work.

Common scenarios

I see one after dark

Do the clean-and-dry routine and monitor the same route for several nights.

I see them in daytime

Treat this as a stronger sign of crowding and arrange professional pest control.

They are near food storage

Discard contaminated open food, clean shelves and use no treatment directly on utensils or food.

Please don’t do this

These shortcuts can make a small problem harder to diagnose or more expensive to repair.

Don’t 1

Fog the whole kitchen

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

Foggers scatter insects and can contaminate food areas; targeted treatment is safer.

Don’t 2

Leave crumbs and bowls overnight

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

A clean-looking counter still needs dry sinks, sealed food and an emptied bin.

Don’t 3

Mix products or use home remedies

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

Never combine pesticides, kerosene, bleach or unlabelled mixtures.

Don’t 4

Seal a busy gap immediately

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

Let treatment reduce activity first, or you may simply push it to another room.

How to keep it from returning

  • Store dry foods in sealed containers.
  • Wipe grease and crumbs after cooking.
  • Dry sinks and repair small leaks promptly.
  • Reduce cardboard stacks near kitchens.
  • Check pipe entries after any plumbing work.
Practical tip
