On a windy evening the bedroom window rattles but the handle will not fully lock. The key turns halfway and stops, as if the frame and lock stopped agreeing.

Quick answer

Check whether the handle moves freely, inspect strike plate alignment, clean and lubricate the mechanism with a dry product, and tighten loose frame screws. Do not force a key or drill the lock.

What usually causes this

The visible symptom is useful, but the small detail around it usually tells the real story.

  • Panel sagged so the latch misses the keep.
  • Dirt or grit jammed in the lock body.
  • Loose handle screws or a worn multipoint latch.

Try these in order

Begin with the reversible checks. The goal is a clean fix, not winning an argument with a stubborn household thing.

Method 1

Try the handle with the window shut

Best for: any stuck lock

Close the panel fully and note whether the handle lifts or turns smoothly.

Method 2

Inspect the strike plate

Best for: latch misses

Look for fresh scrape marks showing where the latch hits the frame.

Method 3

Clean and dry-lube the lock

Best for: grit or stiffness

Use compressed air or a soft brush; apply graphite or silicone sparingly, not thick oil.

Method 4

Tighten hinges and frame screws

Best for: a shifted panel

Check that the sash sits square and fixings are snug without over-tightening.

Method 5

Book window repair

Best for: bent keep or broken cylinder

A pro can realign the keep, replace the cylinder, or adjust multipoint gear.

Kashmir home note

Wind and seasonal movement test sliding and casement locks after monsoon. A lock that worked in dry weather often misses after the frame settles.

What not to do

These shortcuts usually cost more than they save.

Don't 1

Force the key

Can snap the cylinder or strip the lock

A key that stops halfway usually means misalignment, not more strength. Forcing it often breaks the cylinder.

Don't 2

Drill the lock

Damages the frame and rarely fixes alignment

Drilling bypasses the real fault and can leave the profile weak or void warranty.

Don't 3

Replace the whole window

Expensive when the keep is misaligned

Many lock failures are strike plate or panel position issues, not a dead lock body.

Keep it from returning

Fix the small trigger, keep the area clean and dry where relevant, and deal with repeat symptoms before they become a replacement job.

When to book a professional

Book a window professional for a broken key in the cylinder, a lock that will not secure at all, or a panel that will not sit square after adjustment.

FAQs

What is the first check for aluminium window lock not working?

Can I keep using it?

Only if there is no safety risk or spreading damage. Stop when the symptom worsens.

Why did this start suddenly?

A change in load, moisture, wear, cleaning product, or season often exposes a weak point.

Will a temporary fix last?

It can buy time, but repeat symptoms need the cause corrected.
