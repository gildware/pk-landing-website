Quick answer

Blot fresh spills, check the care tag, test any cleaner on a hidden seam, and use minimal moisture. Old odour, large stains, delicate fabric or a soaked cushion needs professional upholstery cleaning.

Methods that actually help — try them in this order

Start with the lowest-risk, reversible method. Move on only if it does not explain or improve the sofa stain or odour.

Method 1

Blot, never scrub

Best for: a new liquid spill

  1. Press a clean white cloth from the outer edge inward; replace it as it absorbs liquid.
  2. Work in good light and take a photo before changing anything.
  3. Change one variable at a time so you can tell what helped.
  4. Stop if the issue worsens or exposes a safety concern.
Method 2

Read the care tag

Best for: an unfamiliar fabric

  1. Check the manufacturer code and removable-cover instructions before using water or solvent.
  2. Work in good light and take a photo before changing anything.
  3. Change one variable at a time so you can tell what helped.
  4. Stop if the issue worsens or exposes a safety concern.
Method 3

Spot-test a mild cleaner

Best for: a small visible mark

  1. Test a tiny amount on a hidden seam, wait for it to dry, then check for colour transfer.
  2. Work in good light and take a photo before changing anything.
  3. Change one variable at a time so you can tell what helped.
  4. Stop if the issue worsens or exposes a safety concern.
Method 4

Clean lightly and dry evenly

Best for: a safe washable fabric

  1. Use minimal suitable solution, dab gently, then ventilate the room and support the cushion shape.
  2. Work in good light and take a photo before changing anything.
  3. Change one variable at a time so you can tell what helped.
  4. Stop if the issue worsens or exposes a safety concern.
Method 5

Book upholstery cleaning

Common scenarios

Tea or coffee just spilled

Blot immediately; do not rub or add several products at once.

The sofa smells musty

Check for a damp cushion, poor airflow or an old spill before deodorising.

The cover is silk, velvet or unlabelled

Avoid DIY wet cleaning and arrange a professional assessment.

Please don’t do this

These shortcuts can make a small problem harder to diagnose or more expensive to repair.

Don’t 1

Scrub a wet stain

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

Hard brushing drives pigment deeper and roughens fibres.

Don’t 2

Use bleach or random solvents

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

They can strip colour, damage backing and leave permanent rings.

Don’t 3

Soak the cushion

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

Too much water can leave odour, mould risk and slow-drying foam.

Don’t 4

Blast it with heat

Best for: avoiding a costly false shortcut

High heat can set protein stains and shrink or distort some fabrics.

How to keep it from returning

  • Use washable throws in high-use seats.
  • Blot spills as they happen.
  • Vacuum seams and under cushions weekly.
  • Air cushions after any damp incident.
  • Keep food and drinks on a stable tray.
Practical tip

Keep a simple note of when and where you first see the sofa stain or odour. That pattern makes a professional visit faster and more useful.

Start by observing the pattern and using the first low-risk method above. Do not jump to a permanent or chemical fix.

Can I fix sofa stain or odour myself?

You can do simple cleaning, observation and basic maintenance when safe. Stop for hidden damage, specialised work or any safety concern.

Why does sofa stain or odour keep returning?
