Why small home jobs are hard to book in Kashmir

In Kashmir, the hardest job to get done is often not the biggest one. Large renovation contracts attract attention because they are more profitable. Small household repairs, preventive maintenance, and short visits are the jobs many families struggle to book quickly, even when the problem is urgent.

  • Technicians may skip a small booking because the visit feels too short or low-value.
  • Homeowners keep old phone numbers that stop working when they need help most.
  • Small leaks, sparks, pests, or clogged drains get postponed until the repair costs much more.
  • Families need one trusted booking path for both a 20-minute fix and a multi-day project.
