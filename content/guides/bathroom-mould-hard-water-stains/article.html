White crust and grey spots gather around a Batamaloo showerhead every few weeks. Scrubbing harder makes the tiles look scratched and the mould returns in the grout lines.

Quick answer

Improve airflow, reduce standing moisture, use the right cleaner for mould vs limescale, and protect grout. Widespread mould, ceiling patches, or mould after a leak need professional cleaning and source repair.

Quick diagnosis map

Start with the symptom, not the most dramatic fix. A small check now can save a cabinet, appliance, or Sunday afternoon.

Stop here

Do not mix bleach and acid cleaners, scrub glazed tiles with metal pads, or paint over mould.

What usually causes this

The visible symptom is useful, but the small detail around it usually tells the real story.

  • Poor ventilation after hot showers.
  • Hard water leaving mineral scale that traps soap.
  • Slow leaks keeping grout damp.

Try these in order

Begin with the reversible checks. The goal is a clean fix, not winning an argument with a stubborn household thing.

Method 1

Dry and ventilate

Best for: after every shower

Run the fan, wipe glass, and leave the door ajar when safe.

Method 2

Match cleaner to stain

Best for: scale vs mould

Use descaler on mineral crust; use mould treatment on organic growth in grout.

Method 3

Test on a tile edge

Best for: strong products

Check that acids or bleach will not etch your finish.

Method 4

Brush grout gently

Best for: light mould

A stiff brush and suitable product beat endless bleach fumes.

Method 5

Book deep cleaning

Best for: heavy buildup

Pros can descale fixtures and treat grout without damaging surfaces.

Take one clear photo before taking anything apart. It helps a professional see the pattern and saves guesswork.

Kashmir home note

Closed bathroom windows through winter keep humidity high in Kashmir flats. Mould and limescale are often a ventilation problem wearing a cleaning disguise.

What not to do

These shortcuts usually cost more than they save.

Don't 1

Mix bleach and acid cleaners

Toxic fumes in small bathroom

Combining bleach with descaler releases dangerous gas in enclosed spaces.

Don't 2

Scrub glazed tiles with metal pads

Scratches finish permanently

Steel wool etches tile glaze and makes future staining worse.

Don't 3

Paint over mould

Mould grows through new coat

Painting without killing mould in grout traps moisture and spreads spores.

Keep it from returning

Fix the small trigger, keep the area clean and dry where relevant, and deal with repeat symptoms before they become a replacement job.

When to book a professional

Book professional cleaning for ceiling mould, large areas, persistent smell, or stains after a bathroom leak.
