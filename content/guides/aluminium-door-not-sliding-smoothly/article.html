The balcony door in a flat needs a hip-check every evening. The glass is fine; the bottom track has collected a season of grit.

Quick answer

Vacuum and wipe the track, clear weep holes, check rollers and alignment, then use a dry silicone lubricant sparingly. Do not force a jammed panel or use thick grease.

What usually causes this

The visible symptom is useful, but the small detail around it usually tells the real story.

  • Dust and grit packed into the bottom channel.
  • Blocked drainage holes holding water and swelling debris.
  • Worn or seized rollers after years of use.

Try these in order

Begin with the reversible checks. The goal is a clean fix, not winning an argument with a stubborn household thing.

Method 1

Vacuum the track

Best for: a dragging panel

Remove loose grit with a vacuum nozzle, then wipe with a soft brush and dry cloth.

Method 2

Clear weep holes

Best for: water in the frame

Find small drainage openings and clear them gently without enlarging.

Method 3

Check rollers and alignment

Best for: persistent scraping

Look for a panel sitting low on one side or a roller that no longer spins.

Method 4

Use dry silicone only

Best for: a clean but sticky track

Apply a window-safe dry lubricant; avoid oil that catches more dust.

Method 5

Book adjustment

Best for: glass stress or lock failure

A pro can realign rollers without stressing the glass or frame.

Kashmir home note

Wind-blown dust and winter grit are hard on sliding doors. A five-minute track clean before the wet season prevents many stuck panels.

What not to do

These shortcuts usually cost more than they save.

Don't 1

Force a jammed panel

Can crack glass or bend the frame

Shoulder-checking a stuck door stresses rollers and can shatter tempered glass. Stop and clean the track first.

Don't 2

Use heavy grease on the track

Attracts dust and makes sticking worse

Thick oil or grease turns grit into paste in the channel. Dry silicone is the window-safe option.

Don't 3

Drill new holes in the frame

Weakens the profile and voids warranty

New holes let water into the frame and rarely fix alignment. Roller adjustment is the proper fix.

Keep it from returning

Fix the small trigger, keep the area clean and dry where relevant, and deal with repeat symptoms before they become a replacement job.

When to book a professional

Book a window professional for cracked glass, a loose frame, a door that will not lock, or persistent leakage after cleaning.

FAQs

What is the first check for aluminium door not sliding smoothly?

Remove loose grit with a vacuum nozzle, then wipe with a soft brush and dry cloth.

Can I keep using it?

Only if there is no safety risk or spreading damage. Stop when the symptom worsens.

Why did this start suddenly?

A change in load, moisture, wear, cleaning product, or season often exposes a weak point.

Will a temporary fix last?

It can buy time, but repeat symptoms need the cause corrected.

When should I book help?
